# Traceable AI for Industrial Decision Making

A framework for making AI-assisted decisions trustworthy in industrial environments — energy, operations, and verification.

## The Problem

Most AI systems tell you what to do. They sound confident. But they cannot show you why, trace how they got there, or tell you when they should not be trusted.

In industrial settings, that is not good enough.

If an AI system influences an operational decision — about energy, equipment, safety, or resource allocation — the output needs to be more than a prediction. It needs to be governable.

## What This Is

This project is a governance framework for AI outputs in high-stakes environments.

Its goal is to make AI reasoning:

- **Traceable** — you can follow how a conclusion was reached
- **Constrained** — the system knows what it is and is not allowed to say
- **Verifiable** — claims can be reviewed, challenged, or rejected
- **Honest about uncertainty** — the system distinguishes evidence from inference from narrative

## Core Principle

No single model is trusted with truth.

Operational conclusions should emerge from constrained reasoning across multiple layers — not from the confidence of one model or one score.

## Who This Is For

Teams building or deploying AI in industrial contexts where decisions have real consequences — energy efficiency, operations management, predictive maintenance, regulatory verification.
