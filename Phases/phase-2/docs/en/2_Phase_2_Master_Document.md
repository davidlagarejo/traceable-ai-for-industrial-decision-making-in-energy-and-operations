## 1. Exact purpose of the subphase
To define the operational constitution of the Decision Core (Phase 2) and formalize its operation over a governed inferential library. Its function is to regulate how the `facility_prior` and its dependencies are transformed into structured, prioritized, and explicitly incomplete analytical units (`Inference Cases`), establishing the limits of breadth, depth, and rarity to avoid noise proliferation.

## 2. Why this subphase matters
It prevents the unjustified epistemic jump and the collapse of the system due to hypothesis overproduction. Without this subphase, the accumulation of Phase 1 data is confused with site truth, and the generative capacity of the LLM is confused with analytical intelligence. It guarantees that any expansion of hypotheses serves a material validation agenda, rather than covering up gaps with narrative or apparent sophistication.

## 3. What is Phase 2 and what is it not
**Phase 2 — Decision Core means:**
A structured analytical transformation layer that takes `facility_prior` + bundles + assumptions + uncertainty markers + source dependencies and converts them into:
- plausible hypotheses,
- prioritized tensions,
- relevant conflicts,
- candidate opportunities,
- visible uncertainty,
- evidence gaps,
- validation agenda,
- and high-value next questions.

**Phase 2 — Decision Core does NOT mean:**
- final recommendations,
- final ROI,
- verification,
- final compliance,
- final investment prioritization,
- savings verification,
- final reporting production,
- or holistic storytelling as a primary product.

## 4. What enters Phase 2 and what does not
**Enters:**
- Construction of plausible operational hypotheses under constraint.
- Identification of structural tensions and conflicts.
- Mapping of conditioned candidate opportunities.
- Formulation of structured validation agendas.
- Explicitation of evidence gaps.

**Does not enter:**
- Final site diagnosis or equipment failure confirmation.
- Definitive investment recommendations.
- Binding compliance declarations.
- Absolute savings estimations.
- Generation of final reporting.

## 7. Governed inferential library
Phase 2 does not generate "ideas" on the fly through free reasoning; it operates by instantiating cases from a **governed inferential library**.
This means that the Decision Core is capable of handling many types of inferential cases, covering obvious, non-obvious, rare, and marginal phenomena, and tensions that even senior experts might omit, composing them into a structured base.
However, this library is not a license for noise. The breadth of the library serves to have coverage over industrial reality, not to produce blind narrative volume.

## 8. What "many cases" does NOT mean
In the context of the inferential library, "many cases" or analytical breadth **does NOT mean**:
- throwing thousands of hypotheses without discipline;
- producing endless lists of ideas;
- throwing in any interesting case even if it has no analytical function;
- using rarities as analytical decoration;
- creating a beautiful but useless ontology;
- hiding a lack of evidence behind breadth.

## 9. What quality means for an Inference Case
Quality CANNOT just mean "it sounds good" or "it is sophisticated". An inference case has operational quality only if it meets these minimums:
- **Real analytical function:** It materially modifies the reading of the site.
- **Condition of applicability:** It is only activated if the prior contains the specific triggers.
- **Explicit dependency:** It is inescapably tied to Phase 1 upstream inputs or bundles.
- **Perturbing capacity:** It can open, restrict, or reorder the active hypothesis set.
- **Validation detonator:** It obligatorily generates or changes a question in the empirical validation agenda.
- **Controlled risk:** It minimizes over-assertion through structural constraints.
- **Degradation:** It has a clear possibility of being downgraded or discarded via evidence.

## 10. What depth means
Depth **does NOT mean** difficult words, long narrative, or more gratuitous layers.
Depth means:
- capturing non-trivial interactions (e.g., how a schedule affects a demand peak);
- connecting systems, practices, tariff restrictions, and climate context;
- detecting second-order tensions;
- preserving uncomfortable but plausible hypotheses that the average benchmark masks;
- or revealing validation questions that were not obvious under superficial inspection.

## 11. What a rare case means
A rare case **does NOT mean** exotic to attract attention, improbable without function, fanciful, or "interesting" just because it's different.
A rare case only deserves to enter the library when:
- it reveals a plausible tension not covered by common cases;
- it materially affects a validation agenda (makes you look for something specific);
- it changes the analytical risk reading or the validation priority under certain conditions;
- or it expands the coverage of the inferential universe without destroying discipline.

## 12. What it means to open a relevant tension
Opening a relevant tension must not be left in vague language. It means:
- introducing a material friction between two or more upstream elements (e.g., thermal requirement vs. local energy cost);
- showing plausible incompatibility between archetype, systems, operation, driver, benchmark, or constraint;
- forcing a question that changes the validation agenda;
- preventing a premature closure by assuming "normal operation";
- or revealing that a simple reading of the site (e.g., "high consumption") was too superficial to explain the underlying mechanics.

## 13. Tension vs. Conflict
To avoid ambiguity in the inferential library, a hard and operational distinction is established:
- **Tension:** A material and probable friction between two elements (e.g., aggressive savings driver vs. 99.9% uptime constraint). It will complicate operation or investment, requires trade-offs, but does not violate absolute physical or logical rules.
- **Conflict:** A hard incompatibility or direct contradiction between the prior and an assumption or benchmark (e.g., the prior declares district steam, but the archetype assumes 100% electric heating). It challenges the validity of upstream data and forces an immediate resolution or a critical error flag.

## 14. Failure Mode: The decorative case
A **decorative case** is a design failure. It is formally defined as an instantiated case that sounds technical or sophisticated, but does not change active hypotheses, does not alter a single point in the Phase 3 validation agenda, and does not restrict the reading of the building. Decorative cases produce analytical smoke and must be algorithmically blocked by demanding a strict `validation_requirement`.

## 15. Main analytical unit
The minimum, governing, and central analytical unit of Phase 2 is the **`Inference Case`**.

**Why it is the correct unit:**
It forces the instantiation of the library by coupling every hypothesis or tension with its base support, its assumptions, its level of uncertainty, and its strict validation requirement. It is the governable atom.

**Why an insight / finding / observation / paragraph is not enough:**
The insight or free narrative encourages over-reading, destroys traceability, and is not computable. A paragraph cannot be evaluated by an admissibility gate.

**Function within a governed inferential library:**
It acts as the modular knowledge contract. The library houses latent cases; Phase 2 actively instantiates them only when the prior justifies it. It allows controlled breadth (many cases in the library) without falling into noise (only applicable ones with a validation agenda are instantiated).

**Minimum conceptual attributes:**
1. `base_support`: Strict traceable link to Phase 1 bundles.
2. `inference_logic`: The pre-approved rule or heuristic from the library justifying the transformation.
3. `claim_type`: Strict taxonomy of the assertion (hypothesis, tension, conflict, etc.).
4. `conditional_statement`: The formulation of the hypothesis or tension under constraint.
5. `dependency_assumptions`: Critical assumptions supporting the inference.
6. `validation_requirement`: What exact Phase 3 data destroys, confirms, or downgrades the case.

**How it prevents collapse into free narrative:**
By requiring a `validation_requirement` and `base_support`, the text generated by the LLM is subordinated to an auditable structure. If the LLM invents something, the `validation_requirement` becomes orphaned from support, and the case is null.

## 16. Permitted claim families
In Phase 2, only conditional, preparatory, and validation-oriented claims are admissible:
- **Plausible hypothesis:** Possible structural assertion given Phase 1 constraints.
- **Tension:** Probable material friction that requires trade-offs.
- **Conflict:** Hard contradiction requiring data resolution.
- **Candidate opportunity:** Improvement pathway conditioned upon validation of a critical assumption.
- **Uncertainty:** Declaration of multiple possible states without resolution.
- **Evidence gap:** Absence of a data point that materially blocks analytical advancement.
- **Validation priority:** Ordering of which gaps have the highest impact if resolved.

## 17. Prohibited claim families
They are epistemically illegal and cause architectural failure in Phase 2:
- **Recommendation** (final or unconditional).
- **Diagnosis** (assertion of empirical site failure).
- **Closed causal claim** (establishing causality without Phase 3 validation).
- **Savings claim** (absolute estimation of dollars or kWh saved).
- **Final compliance claim** (legal certification).

## 18. Epistemological statute of Phase 2
Phase 2 operates strictly under a preparatory decision grade (**Decision-grade limit**).
Its operational function is **restricted inferential expansion oriented towards a validation agenda**.
It does not emit site truths, physical diagnoses, or terminal recommendations. Its design objective is not to resolve uncertainty, but to map what is analytically possible under known constraints, translating information gaps into structured requirements for Phase 3.

## 19. Boundary with Phase 1
- Phase 1 constructs the `facility_prior` (the given, public, immutable).
- Phase 2 does not redesign or correct the provenance of Phase 1.
- However, Phase 2 **can** reorder, tension, degrade, or condition the reading of the prior through its inferential cases (e.g., pointing out a conflict between two inherited inputs).

## 20. Boundary with Phase 3
- Phase 2 generates the `Inference Case` that triggers the `validation_agenda`.
- Phase 3 (if it exists) executes the agenda to collect site evidence.
- Phase 2 reaches strictly up to the structured question formulation; Phase 3 provides the empirical answer that downgrades, confirms, or destroys the claim.

## 21. Boundary with Phase 4
- Phase 4 is the final reporting engine, definitive recommendation, and high decision-weight output.
- Phase 2 is explicitly forbidden to behave as final reporting or to pass its hypotheses as resolved conclusions to Phase 4.

## 22. Illustrative minimum examples

**1. Common case vs. rare useful case:**
- *Common case:* Pre-1990 office building; plausible hypothesis of predominant fluorescent lighting.
- *Rare useful case:* Industrial use declares fast freezing; activates tension if tariff is coincident peak (impossibility of simple load-shedding).

**2. Rare useful case vs. rare-smoke case (decorative):**
- *Rare useful case:* 1960 chemical plant; hypothesis of steam traps purging to drain. Material tension for Phase 3 agenda.
- *Rare-smoke case:* Possible anomalous magnetic resonance in condensate. Does not alter material validation.

**3. Deep case vs. inflated case:**
- *Deep case:* User declares 9-5 schedule, but archetype suggests 24/7 ventilation. Second-order tension between savings driver and operational restriction.
- *Inflated case:* "The holistic thermodynamic paradigm suggests suboptimal interaction." (Gibberish).

**4. Tension vs. Conflict:**
- *Tension:* Extreme efficiency target versus an infrastructure dependent on old chillers.
- *Conflict:* The facility_prior says "100% electric use" but a regulatory flag indicates "sanctioned gas boiler on site".

**5. Case that opens a relevant tension vs. case that changes nothing:**
- *Opens tension:* Client seeks "aggressive savings", but site is Tier III Data Center (100% uptime constraint). Every retrofit requires N+1 redundancy.
- *Changes nothing:* "The client wants to save money and servers use electricity."

**6. Plausible hypothesis vs. diagnosis:**
- *Plausible hypothesis:* EUI 40% higher than average. Hypothesis: Possible simultaneous heating and cooling due to miscalibrated BMS. Requires validation.
- *Diagnosis (Prohibited):* "The BMS is broken and valves are stuck."

**7. Candidate opportunity vs. final recommendation:**
- *Candidate opportunity:* Opportunity for base load reduction by repairing compressed air leaks, subject to ultrasound audit.
- *Final recommendation (Prohibited):* "Change all quick disconnects tomorrow."

## 23. Exact decisions that remain closed
- Phase 2 operates strictly over a governed inferential library.

- Quality, depth, and rarity are measured exclusively by their material impact on the validation agenda.
- The described outputs are mandatory conceptual families, not suggestions.
- Diagnoses, final recommendations, savings calculations, and final reports are formally prohibited.

## 1. Exact purpose of the subphase
To define the flow architecture that converts the static prior of Phase 1 into an ecosystem of active inferences in Phase 2. It formalizes how the governed inferential library awakens, instantiating latent cases under strict applicability rules, and defines the conceptual contract of the structured objects that contain and relate those inferences without violating the *Decision-grade* limit.

## 2. Why this subphase matters
Without this structured pipeline, the "inferential library" defined in 2A would be an inert repository or a menu from which the LLM would choose freely, hallucinating connections. This subphase guarantees that every jump from public data to the validation agenda is mechanically traced, preventing the accumulation of cases from simulating depth or collapsing into an unauditable narrative.

## 3. Exact function of 2B
2B closes the conceptual engineering gap between the inherited structure of Phase 1 and the active inferential structure of Phase 2. It acts as the logical orchestrator: it defines the triggering rules (activation), the knowledge vessel (the `Inference Case` contract), and the output containers (structured objects) that order the analysis before any weight evaluation (scoring).

## 4. What 2B does NOT do
This subphase does **NOT**:
- define final scoring for confidence or impact of hypotheses;
- set mathematical survival thresholds for claims;
- execute final output gates towards Phase 3 or 4;
- perform site verification;
- formulate investment recommendations;
- generate final reporting in natural language;
- implement software, SQL, or technical pipelines in code.

## 5. What this subphase must exactly produce
1. The flow model (pipeline) connecting inputs to library activation.
2. The strict conceptual contract of the `Inference Case`.
3. The closed list of mandatory structured objects in Phase 2.
4. The logic of relationship (inheritance) and composition between those objects, ensuring that the sum of weak hypotheses does not create a strong diagnosis.

## 6. Minimum logical pipeline of Phase 2
The inference flow operates sequentially under this contract:
1. **Ingestion:** Reception of bundles and the Phase 1 prior.
2. **Trigger Evaluation:** Contrast of the dependencies demanded by each latent case in the library against the prior data.
3. **Instantiation:** Transformation of latent cases with satisfied triggers into activated cases.
4. **Packaging:** Each activated case is formalized into an explicit `Inference Case`.
5. **Classification and Composition:** The `Inference Cases` are grouped by family (hypothesis, tension, conflict, opportunity, gap) and projected onto the structured output registers.
6. **Agenda Distillation:** The validation requirements of all live cases are unified and prioritized in the `validation_queue` and translated into `next_best_questions`.

## 7. Latent case vs. activated case
- **Latent Case:** Resides inert in the governed library. It is a general and reusable knowledge rule (e.g., "If old building + cold climate + high night consumption = probable setback failure"). It does not apply to a specific site and has no active analytical weight.
- **Activated Case:** It is a latent case whose minimum applicability conditions have been satisfied (or plausibly satisfied via traceable assumptions) by the current `facility_prior`. It becomes a live `Inference Case` and becomes part of the site's analytical model. Activation **does not equate** to empirical assertion or final survival; it equates to "deserves to be investigated and validated".

## 8. Case activation logic
Activation is mechanical, not creative. A case only awakens if it meets:
- **Applicability conditions (Triggers):** The prior must contain the key variables required by the latent case (e.g., `facility_type == 'Food Processing'` AND `climate_zone == '4A'`).
- **Minimum dependencies present:** The case must be able to anchor its logic to a specific Phase 1 input, bundle, or asset hypothesis.
- **Epistemological compatibility:** The activation of the case must not explicitly contradict a previously admitted hard constraint (unless the case is explicitly designed to document a conflict).
- **Potential analytical value:** Its activation must force a downstream action (change perceived risk, modify the validation agenda, or restrict other hypotheses).

## 9. Conceptual contract of the Inference Case
Every activated `Inference Case` must carry, at a minimum and non-negotiably, the following conceptual fields to prevent collapse into free narrative:
1. `case_id`: Unique identifier inherited from the latent library.
2. `base_support_traces`: Exact links to the Phase 1 bundles or variables that triggered activation.
3. `claim_family`: Typological classification (tension, hypothesis, opportunity, gap, conflict).
4. `conditional_statement`: The analytical assertion drafted under constraint ("Given [X], [Y] is probable").
5. `dependency_assumptions`: Assumptions made to allow instantiation (e.g., "Assuming typical 24/7 operation for the subsector").
6. `validation_requirement`: The exact empirical data point, measurement, or document that will be demanded in Phase 3 to confirm, downgrade, or destroy this case. Without this field, the case is null.

## 10. Mandatory structured objects in Phase 2
Phase 2 must instantiate and populate these objects. They are the operational containers of active knowledge:
- `inference_case_register`: The central repository and root object of all activated cases for the site.
- `hypothesis_register`: Subset of claims about the probable physical, operational, behavioral state, action barriers, or organizational capacities, always under *Decision-grade* constraint.
- `tension_map`: Subset of claims revealing probable frictions that will require trade-offs (e.g., operation vs. design, tariff vs. client driver).
- `conflict_register`: Cases where hard material incompatibilities exist. Not just data contradictions (e.g., prior vs. benchmark), but structural operational collisions: aggressive savings driver vs. extreme continuity constraint, electrification opportunity vs. uncertain main electrical capacity, or compliance pressure vs. insufficient operational control.
- `opportunity_candidate_matrix`: Improvement pathways strictly conditioned upon the resolution of Phase 3 assumptions.
- `uncertainty_register`: Map of open states, systemic ambiguities, plurality of valid explanations, or epistemological limits where the model recognizes that multiple configurations are equally probable under current information.
- `evidence_gap_register`: Strict register of concrete missing evidence, telemetry, or data that materially blocks the advancement of a claim.
- `validation_queue`: Structured object that logically prioritizes and orders the `validation_requirements` of all live cases.
- `next_best_questions`: Operational translation of the `validation_queue` into specific and executable audit questions or actions in Phase 3.

## 11. Relationship between objects
- The **`inference_case_register` is the operational root object**. No downstream object can contain information not originating from a formally instantiated `Inference Case` in the root register.
- `hypothesis_register`, `tension_map`, `conflict_register`, `opportunity_candidate_matrix`, `uncertainty_register`, and `evidence_gap_register` are **filtered and enriched views** (derivations) of the root register based on `claim_family`.
- The `validation_queue` is the **analytical sink**. It aggregates, de-duplicates, and structures the logical dependency of the `validation_requirements`. The `next_best_questions` derive from this queue.
- **Law of Strict Inheritance:** No derived object (e.g., `opportunity_candidate_matrix`) can remove uncertainty labels, ignore assumptions, or artificially increase the epistemological strength of underlying `Inference Cases`.

## 12. Difference between output objects (Expanded Claim Typology)
- **Live hypothesis:** "The building is compatible with an absence or low granularity of BMS control over the secondary pumps." (Describes a plausible mechanical or operational state).
- **Tension:** "The requirement for high indoor air quality causes friction with the high peak-hour demand cost." (Friction that complicates operation, requires trade-offs, but does not violate hard rules).
- **Conflict:** "The client has an extreme CAPEX reduction driver, but regulation demands a comprehensive heating retrofit within 3 years." (Structural collision between driver and regulatory constraint; blocks prioritization until resolved).
- **Candidate opportunity:** "Possible switch to VAV, *assuming* validation of duct compatibility." (Improvement blocked by an assumption).
- **Uncertainty:** "It is unknown whether the night demand peak is due to an undeclared continuous industrial process or a BMS programming failure; both explain the consumption." (Plurality of open states).
- **Evidence gap:** "The chiller time schedule log is required." (Concrete missing data point).
- **Validation priority (Queue):** "Obtaining the chiller log is Priority 1 because it resolves the state of night uncertainty and unblocks two candidate opportunities."
- **Next question:** "Can you export a 7-day report of the main chiller's runtime history?" (Translation to audit action).

## 13. Case composition logic
Composition is the mechanics of crossing isolated `Inference Cases` to extract disciplined holistic readings, without resorting to free narrative. It is structured in:
- **Convergence:** Three different cases demand the same `validation_requirement` (e.g., check the electrical panel). That gap ascends to critical priority in the `validation_queue`.
- **Cluster by system:** Grouping activated cases about "compressed air" (leaks, pressure, hours of use) to formulate a coherent Phase 3 audit block.
- **Validation chain (Common dependency):** Case A (heat pump opportunity) depends on Case B (electrical capacity hypothesis). B becomes the strict precondition of A.
- **Cluster by tension (Emergent tension):** One case suggests high base demand. Another case indicates a tariff with maximum demand penalties. Their composition activates a higher Tension ("Structural risk of cost overrun due to lack of base demand control") that isolatedly was not obvious.

## 14. Limits of composition
To prevent epistemic jumps and analytical smoke, composition has absolute prohibitions:
- **Forbidden to inflate epistemological strength:** Five hypotheses pointing to the same problem do not turn into a closed empirical diagnosis. They still require validation.
- **Forbidden to turn opportunity into recommendation:** Composing an opportunity with a favorable ROI case does not eliminate the Phase 3 technical feasibility requirement.
- **Forbidden to simulate depth by volume:** Mixing cases without a material connection to produce dense paragraphs is blocked. Composition is only valid if it restricts hypotheses, reveals tensions, or alters the order of the `validation_queue`.
- **Forbidden to hide conflict:** If composition reveals contradictory cases (e.g., one suggests over-ventilation and another under-ventilation due to comfort complaints), it is not narratively averaged. An explicit Conflict is declared and moved as an urgency to the validation agenda.

## 15. Basic logic of structural discard and degradation
The pipeline includes destructive mechanisms to control analytical noise:
- **Non-activation:** The vast majority of latent cases simply do not awaken because they do not meet the prior's triggers.
- **Survival:** The case meets triggers, annotates its assumptions, and passes to the root register, feeding the validation agenda.
- **Activation and composition degradation:** A case activates (e.g., solar panel opportunity), but an inherited `improvement_constraint_profile` (e.g., "structurally compromised roof") immediately degrades it to "Provisionally blocked" or "Not prioritizable under current conditions", preventing it from contaminating the agenda of viable opportunities without declaring it "Unfeasible" (which would be a premature verificatory closure).
- **Activation towards conflict:** Two cases logically collide. They lose their status as useful hypotheses and degrade to "Conflict", demanding urgent empirical resolution.
- **Survival as a pure question:** Many activated cases do not end up as improvement opportunities; their life cycle concludes by justifying the inclusion of a critical question in the `next_best_questions`.

## 16. Illustrative minimum examples

**1. OFFICE / COMPLIANCE-DRIVEN (NYC, Old, Commercial, Unknown BMS)**
- *Activation:* Latent case "risk of regulatory penalty due to old vintage" activates.
- *Emergent tension:* Material friction between the "imminent compliance pressure" and the "insufficient operational control" derived from the BMS evidence gap.
- *Agenda:* "Verify existence and capacity of BMS" enters the `validation_queue` as top priority.

**2. FOOD PROCESSING / REFRIGERATION / RELIABILITY (24/7, Critical Uptime, Cost)**
- *Differential Activation:* Office "night setback" cases do not activate. "Continuous condensation efficiency" cases activate.
- *Material Conflict:* "Savings by capacity reduction" case collides with "extreme 24/7 continuity" constraint.
- *Impact:* The shutdown opportunity is degraded to "Not prioritizable"; composition directs the search towards thermal storage.

**3. STEAM / CONDENSATE (Thermal process, Old vintage)**
- *Activation:* The prior triggers the "hypothesis of losses or operational degradation in the steam distribution system" case.
- *Limit:* The system does NOT diagnose "you are losing $50,000 from failed traps".
- *Agenda:* Instantiates a candidate opportunity and translates the need to the next question: "Is there an active ultrasonic steam trap audit plan?".

**4. COMPRESSED AIR (Cost, Extended hours)**
- *Activation:* Industry + cost driver = activates "air leaks optimization" case.
- *Epistemic Limit:* Opportunity strictly conditioned to Phase 3.
- *Agenda:* The `validation_queue` demands "evaluation of consumption during non-productive hours".

**5. POWER QUALITY (Many old motors, High cost signal)**
- *Quality vs. Decoration:* No paragraph about "motors and environment" is drafted. An analytical case activates: "Probable tariff penalty due to low power factor".
- *Agenda:* "Can you provide interval billing to review kVAR charges?".

**6. CASE COMPOSITION (Electrification + Peak Tariff + Age)**
- *Composition:* A "switch to heat pump" opportunity (A) is crossed with a "pre-1980 age" constraint (B) and a "winter peak tariff" (C).
- *Result:* A material Conflict is generated ("Opportunity vs. operational unfeasibility due to uncertain electrical capacity"). The electrification opportunity remains "Provisionally blocked" until the validation agenda resolves the actual capacity of the main panel.

## 17. Typical errors 2B must block
- Allowing latent cases to flood the analysis without passing through the mechanical applicability gate (triggers).
- Treating the `Inference Case` as unstructured narrative and omitting the `validation_requirement` field.
- Reducing the `conflict_register` to simple data errors (e.g., a typo in the year) and forgetting structural operational conflicts (e.g., savings vs. continuity).
- Collapsing `uncertainty_register` (valid ambiguities) with `evidence_gap_register` (hard missing data).
- Collapsing `validation_queue` (logical structure of dependencies) with `next_best_questions` (final operational formulation).
- Using verificatory closure language (like "Unfeasible Opportunity") instead of proportional modifiers ("Provisionally blocked", "Not prioritizable").
- Using composition to accumulate weak clues and falsely declare them as a diagnosis or recommendation.

## 18. Exact decisions that 2B leaves closed
- The inference flow (pipeline) is rigid: ingestion → activation → packaged instantiation → classification/composition → agenda.
- A latent case is inert; it only acquires analytical weight by becoming an activated `Inference Case` that meets conditions.
- The `Inference Case` contract demands non-negotiable support dependencies and validation requirements.
- The `inference_case_register` is the operational root object; the matrices of hypotheses, tensions, opportunities, uncertainties, conflicts, and gaps derive strictly from it.
- Composition seeks convergence in validation, common dependencies, and second-order tensions, never the fabrication of certainties.
- Structural degradation is indispensable: a case can activate and remain provisionally blocked without requiring deep empirical evaluation if an inherited constraint invalidates it.

## 19. Completion criterion for 2B
Subphase 2B is considered operationally and conceptually closed by having unambiguously set:
- The sequential inference pipeline.
- The mechanical activation rules.
- The absolute distinction between latent case and activated `Inference Case`.
- The contract of minimum fields for the case.
- The minimum mandatory structured objects and their inheritance relations.
- The functional separation between uncertainty, evidence gaps, validation queue, and next questions.
- The operational breadth of the conflict register (including structural and business collisions).
- The composition rules for holistic reading and their express prohibitions against the epistemic jump.
- The proportionate mechanics of discard and degradation (provisional blocking) that prevent premature verificatory closure.
# 2C — Minimum Scoring, Transformation Gates, Differential Survival, and Degradation Rules

## 1. Exact purpose of the subphase
To establish the logical and normative engine that decides the lifecycle of an `Inference Case` once activated. This subphase strictly defines how a case survives, transforms, degrades, or is discarded, guaranteeing that the Phase 2 analytical ecosystem maintains epistemological breadth without collapsing into noise, hallucination, or premature convergence.

## 2. Why this subphase matters
Because the activation of cases from a prior (Phase 2B) generates multiplicity. Without an implacable quality control mechanism, Phase 2 would suffer a combinatorial explosion of hypotheses or, worse, a hierarchization based on the narrative eloquence of the LLM. This subphase ensures that a minimal, rich, and structured set of cases survives, where justified rarity coexists with central robustness, and where no weak claim can masquerade as certainty through score accumulation.

## 3. Exact function of 2C
To provide the toll system (binary gates), relative evaluation (minimum scoring), taxonomic categorization (differential survival), and structural routing (transformation rules) that processes each activated case and places it in its final state within Phase 2 downstream objects, guaranteeing the strict preservation of uncertainty and conflicts.

## 4. What 2C does NOT do
Phase 2C does NOT verify site reality.
It does NOT calculate truth.
It does NOT set strong probabilistic confidence or statistical certainty intervals.
It does NOT produce ROI calculations.
It does NOT convert plausibility into empirical certainty.
It does NOT replace field validation.
It does NOT implement dozens of theatrical scores to simulate analytical sophistication.

## 5. What exactly this subphase must close
1. The confirmation that scoring is strictly auxiliary.
2. The definition of the only three admissible scores.
3. The definitive list of non-negotiable binary gates.
4. The differential survival taxonomy.
5. The final states a case can adopt.
6. The absolute law of non-compensation between scores and gates.
7. The inheritance rules towards downstream objects.

## 6. Real need for scoring in Phase 2
In Phase 2, scoring is necessary solely and exclusively for *relative prioritization and agenda sequencing*, not for truth validation. A high score does not mean "this is true"; it means "this is urgent to validate" or "this has high structural impact if it turns out to be true". Scoring exists to order the output towards Phase 3 (field), not to replace empirical verification.

## 7. Recommended minimum scores
Only three continuous numerical evaluation vectors (e.g., 0.0 - 1.0) are admitted:

1. **`plausibility_score`**: Exclusively measures the legitimacy of keeping the case alive within the *Decision-grade* based on its structural traceability with the prior and the base support. It DOES NOT measure empirical truth probability or causal certainty.
   - *Enables:* Entry into the survival zone.
   - *Penalizes:* Logical leaps disconnected from physical data or system phenomenology.
2. **`decision_relevance_score`**: Measures the systemic, economic, or operational impact if the hypothesis were to be true. A case can have a very high relevance level and still be discarded if it fails a critical gate.
   - *Enables:* Prioritization of cases that move the needle for the business or operation.
   - *Penalizes:* Technical triviality and decorative perfectionism without real impact.
3. **`validation_urgency_score`**: Measures the operational cost and risk of remaining in ignorance regarding this case.
   - *Enables:* Top-down ordering of the `validation_queue`.
   - *Penalizes:* Interesting cases whose postponement does not alter short- or medium-term risk.

## 8. Scores that must be explicitly prohibited
The following are blocked for life in the architecture:
- `truth_score` (false sovereignty).
- `certainty_score` (illusion of empirical resolution without being on-site).
- `confidence_score` (if used as a truth metric instead of a data support metric).
- `holistic_score` (inscrutable black box).
- `intelligence_score` (decorative metric with no operational function).

## 9. Minimum binary gates of Phase 2
Critical admissibility is governed exclusively by boolean gates (`TRUE`/`FALSE`). If a case fails a gate, its score is irrelevant.

1. `has_base_support`: Is there direct traceability to the Phase 1 prior?
2. `assumptions_explicit`: Are the assumptions sustaining the case declared?
3. `uncertainty_explicit`: Is what is not known exactly declared?
4. `validation_requirement_present`: Does the case define what physical evidence would prove or refute it?
5. `not_diagnostic_language`: Does the case avoid stating that "X is the definitive problem"?
6. `not_recommendation_language`: Does the case avoid prescribing "Y must be done"?
7. `epistemically_compatible`: Does the case respect the physical laws, engineering principles, AND the epistemological statute of Phase 2 (i.e., it is not a disguised diagnosis or a causal closure)?
8. `non_decorative_case`: Does the case provide an investigative vector not covered by existing cases?
9. `composed_output_not_stronger_than_inputs`: In case of composition, does the output maintain the uncertainty level of the weakest input?
10. `conflict_preserved_if_present`: If it collides with another valid hypothesis, is the collision explicitly documented?

## 10. Non-compensation rule
It is established as a fundamental axiom of the Decision Core: **No high score compensates for the failure of an epistemological gate.**
A case with a 99% `decision_relevance_score` that fails the `not_diagnostic_language` gate (because it assumes closed causality) is immediately discarded. Excellence in priority does not exempt from epistemic discipline.

## 11. Differential survival of cases
Phase 2 does not seek "the single best hypothesis"; it seeks an ecosystem of hypotheses encompassing the plausible probability space. Therefore, not all cases are treated, evaluated, or discarded with the same aggressiveness.

- **Core Cases:**
  - *Function:* Form the backbone of the investigation. High plausibility, high impact.
  - *Survival:* Survive by default. Dictate immediate validation priorities.
  - *Quick death criterion:* They die if their formulation is redundant, incompatible with the base support, or not validable based on physical collection.
  
- **Secondary Cases:**
  - *Function:* Provide systemic context. Medium plausibility, medium impact.
  - *Survival:* Survive conditionally. They exist to prevent narrowing the field team's vision.
  - *Quick death criterion:* They die if the general volume of activated cases is too high and their differential contribution is marginal compared to core cases.
  
- **Useful Rare Cases:**
  - *Function:* Expose hidden systemic risks, edge failures, or counter-intuitive tensions. Low/medium plausibility, extreme urgency if they turn out to be true.
  - *Survival:* Survive *thanks* to their asymmetry. Their function is to force an uncomfortable question onto the agenda.
  - *Quick death criterion:* They die instantly if they do not have an actionable `validation_requirement`. A rare case that is also impossible to verify empirically is pure noise.

## 12. Minimum states or destinations of an Inference Case
A case activated in Phase 2B can only end up in one of these logical states:
1. `not_activated` (ignored by the prior).
2. `activated_pending_evaluation` (transitory state).
3. `alive_as_hypothesis` (passes gates, goes to register).
4. `alive_as_tension` (passes gates, partially collides with prior or another variable).
5. `alive_as_conflict` (passes gates, is mutually exclusive with another live hypothesis).
6. `alive_as_opportunity_candidate` (passes gates, points to theoretical improvement under hard constraint, BUT does not operate as a recommendation or investment instruction).
7. `degraded` (remains alive within the ecosystem, but relegated to the bottom of the hierarchy as contingent context).
8. `discarded` (fails gates, is noise or total redundancy).
9. `translated_to_gap` (dies as an active *claim*; transitions to exist solely as a record that critical material evidence is missing, blocking analysis).
10. `translated_to_question` (dies as an affirmative *claim* and mutates into a direct inquiry prompt for data collection in Phase 3).
11. `escalated_to_validation_priority` (high urgency, heads the queue).

## 13. Minimum transformation rules
- Becomes **Live Hypothesis** if: Passes all gates and has a clear falsifiable formulation.
- Becomes **Tension/Conflict** if: Two live cases project opposing vectors on the same subsystem. The system wraps them in a conflict object instead of forcing a winner.
- Becomes **Opportunity Candidate** if: The case demonstrates a plausible inefficiency, BUT the transformation obligatorily injects a physical or business `constraint`. It is never presented as an intervention recommendation.
- Becomes **Gap/Question** if: The case passes gates, but its empirical validation in the current state of information is impossible. The affirmative mode is aborted and it is expelled to the Phase 3 requirement lists.
- Becomes **Discarded** if: Fails critical gates, its existence is redundant, or its language incurs in closed diagnosis or recommendation.

## 14. What must happen with downstream objects
The resulting objects (`hypothesis_register`, `tension_map`, `conflict_register`, `opportunity_candidate_matrix`, `validation_queue`) **inherit without alteration the epistemic limits of the cases that compose them.**
- A `conflict_register` cannot resolve the conflict to deliver a "clean" output. It must deliver the collision intact.
- An `opportunity_candidate_matrix` cannot hide the `validation_requirement`. The uncertainty condition is printed alongside the opportunity.
- The aggregation or composition of cases to populate these objects is never allowed to inflate the original certainty. If three low-plausibility cases are joined, the resulting cluster remains low plausibility.

## 15. Minimal illustrative examples

1. **Rare but decorative case → discarded:**
   "The vibration could be due to a quantum resonance of the materials under atypical thermal stress." (Fails `has_base_support` and `epistemically_compatible`. Immediate discard).

2. **Deep but highly conditioned case → survives degraded (Secondary):**
   "Plausible sync tension if sensor A has a 2ms lag against the PLC scan." (Passes gates, but `decision_relevance` is low. Survives degraded at the bottom of the register in case everything else fails).

3. **Opportunity candidate with hard constraint → is not recommendation:**
   "Reducing compressor pressure by 2 bar is a plausible efficiency scenario." (Survives as an opportunity, but the system injects the constraint: "Requires validating cavitation limit in downstream pump before it can be considered").

4. **Strong composition with hidden conflict → fails gate:**
   Two cases on airflow are composed to make an overarching thermal optimization case, but one assumes induced draft and the other forced draft. (Fails `conflict_preserved_if_present`. The macro-case is destroyed and individual cases are exposed in the `conflict_register`).

5. **Core Case vs Secondary Case vs Useful Rare Case:**
   - *Core:* "The observed pressure drop is compatible with an obstruction in the filtration train (priority hypothesis to validate)."
   - *Secondary:* "A deviation in the transmitter calibration would explain the same pressure drop reading."
   - *Rare Useful:* "Operational tension: the mechanical bypass valve reported being jammed during the last shutdown. If it remains semi-open, the pressure drop is misleading." (Low probability, but extremely high urgency for physical confirmation).

6. **1–2 cases vs 5–7 non-redundant cases:**
   If the system activates only "Electrical failure" and "Mechanical failure" (2 cases), it has simplified prematurely. If it activates 5-7 cases exploring specific components, control logics, sensor failures, and operational constraints, it achieves governed breadth. If it generates 50 variations of a problem, it is redundant noise and quick death criteria must be applied.

## 16. Typical errors that 2C must block
- Creating a `truth_score` or `probability_score` to fake analytical rigor.
- Allowing a "high intelligence/relevance" score to save a case that violates non-diagnostic language (compensation).
- Transforming `opportunity_candidates` into prescriptions by removing uncertainty to "please the client".
- Eliminating useful rare cases just because their initial plausibility is low, killing the discovery of asymmetric risks.
- Allowing inferential breadth to justify the passage of exotic noise or verbiage without support.
- Hiding conflicts under a smoothed narrative when populating the `tension_map`.

## 17. Exact decisions that 2C leaves closed
- The score set is strictly limited to 3, oriented towards relevance and validation, not empirical truth.
- Survival, discard, and transformation decisions rest on a rigid system of 10 binary gates.
- The non-compensation rule between scores and gates is absolute.
- Differential survival (Core, Secondary, Useful Rare) is institutionalized and has quick death criteria.
- The inheritance of restrictions towards downstream objects is mandatory and without loss of uncertainty.

## 18. Completion criteria for 2C
This subphase is considered closed upon having formal operational definitions for:
- The strict set of minimum scores.
- The critical survival gates and the non-negotiable rule of non-compensation.
- The differential survival categorization with quick death criteria.
- The precise final destinations of the `Inference Case` (`degraded`, `translated_to_gap`, etc.).
- The strict inheritance of uncertainty restrictions towards all Phase 2 downstream objects.

# 2D — Epistemological Control Mechanisms of the Decision Core

## 1. Exact purpose of the subphase
The purpose of subphase 2D is to establish the unbreakable semantic and structural boundaries of the Decision Core. This subphase ensures that the system restricts elegant over-assertion, guaranteeing that no inferential object generated in previous phases (2A, 2B, 2C) acquires a degree of certainty, causality, or prescriptiveness that exceeds its actual evidentiary support from Phase 1.

## 2. Why this subphase matters
Because inferential breadth and the composition of multiple latent cases create a systemic risk of structural hallucination. Without Phase 2D, the assembly of valid but conditional inferences could narratively mutate into a certain diagnosis or a prescriptive recommendation, violating the principles of Phase 0. This subphase matters because it guarantees that the framework's intelligence does not destroy its evidentiary discipline.

## 3. Exact function of 2D
Phase 2D operates as an epistemological containment barrier. It does not add inferential power nor does it improve the technical content of the cases; it strictly controls the way `Inference Cases` and their derived objects (`hypothesis_register`, `tension_map`, etc.) can be interpreted, composed, preserved, and translated into natural language. It acts as the semantic regulator that neuters any attempt by the system to sound more certain than it structurally is.

## 4. What 2D does NOT do
- It DOES NOT redefine the foundational principles of Phase 0.
- It DOES NOT redesign the analytical architecture, the inferential library, or the scoring mechanisms of phases 2A, 2B, or 2C.
- It DOES NOT add more scores or evaluation metrics.
- It DOES NOT implement software, databases, or processing architectures.
- It DOES NOT replace the organization's writing style document.
- It DOES NOT produce reports, dashboards, or user interfaces.

## 5. What exactly this subphase must close
This subphase normatively closes the explicit blocking of illegitimate inferential leaps (benchmark → diagnosis, proxy → truth, hypothesis → conclusion, composition → certainty, narrative → analysis, LLM → sovereignty). It also closes the mandatory standard for the unaltered preservation of uncertainty and material conflict, defines the exact grammar of permitted assertions, and establishes the escalation protocol when logical structure is insufficient.

## 6. Anti benchmark → diagnosis policy
A benchmark is an external statistical reference, never local evidence of state. A deviated benchmark only has permission to trigger investigation, activate latent cases, open operational tensions, or prioritize questions in the `validation_queue`. It is strictly forbidden for a benchmark to transform into a claim of actual operation, deficiency, or diagnosis of the physical site.

## 7. Anti proxy → truth policy
Constructs derived from Phase 1 (plausible systems, plausible practices, candidate archetypes, inferred constraints, or declared drivers) are not equivalent to confirmed truth. They are exclusively initialization proxies. These elements only authorize the activation of cases or the formulation of a conditioned hypothesis. Writing an operational proxy as a confirmed fact of the site constitutes a severe epistemological violation.

## 8. Anti hypothesis → conclusion policy
A strong hypothesis, regardless of its activation level or survival in Phase 2C gates, remains a hypothesis. The policy mandates explicitly and permanently preserving its conditionality, its dependency chain, and its strict requirement for empirical validation. No Phase 2 process can remove the "hypothesis" label to replace it with "conclusion".

## 9. Anti composition → certainty policy
The aggregation of multiple cases does not automatically elevate the level of certainty. Five evidentiary weaknesses do not add up to create an empirical strength. Case composition is permitted solely and exclusively to demonstrate thematic convergence, reveal an emerging tension, evidence a concentration of gaps, or reorder the validation agenda. The production of a compound diagnosis using the volume of cases as a simulator of empirical truth is prohibited.

## 10. Anti narrative → analysis policy
Narrative in Phase 2 has no structural generation capacity; it is purely a translational function. All generated text must have a strict isomorphism with a pre-existing structured object (`inference_case_register`, `tension_map`, etc.). If there is no upstream structured object to support a statement, the corresponding downstream sentence must not exist. Prose cannot fill analytical voids.

## 11. Anti sovereign LLM policy
The LLM operates exclusively as a subordinate semantic orchestrator.
- **It can:** Translate structured objects into permitted natural language, extract explicit dependencies from input texts, format logical collisions into tension maps.
- **It cannot:** Invent case activations, resolve logical conflicts by intuition, discard contradictory evidence, generate conclusions not derived from an explicit `Inference Case`, or act with analytical sovereignty over the truth of the site.

## 12. Uncertainty preservation policy
Unresolved material uncertainty is not a system defect; it is a legitimate and critical operational output. It is mandatory to keep all uncertainty explicit, inherit it intact to downstream objects, and translate it formally to the `evidence_gap_register` or `next_best_questions`. Attempting to erase uncertainty in favor of apparent clarity or executive readability is prohibited.

## 13. Conflict preservation policy
Faced with a logical collision between two or more robust `Inference Cases`, the directive is to preserve the friction. It is mandatory to declare the conflict in the `conflict_register`, degrade the assertion level of both involved cases, and elevate the urgency of their empirical validation on site. It is strictly forbidden to average results, choose the most harmonious case, or smooth over contradictions using narrative artifices.

## 14. Permitted and prohibited language policy
Semantic control is non-negotiable. The following restrictive grammar is established:

**Permitted Language (Conditional and Preparatory):**
- "compatible with"
- "plausible"
- "conditioned on"
- "requires validating"
- "opens a tension"
- "suggests"
- "cannot be ruled out"
- "remains uncertain"
- "blocked by lack of evidence"

**Prohibited Language (Deterministic and Conclusive):**
- "is"
- "the cause is"
- "must be implemented"
- "is failing"
- "the main problem is"
- "the expected savings is"
- "complies / fails to comply" (in a final sense)
- "the best decision is"

## 15. Escalation / override policy
When the inferential structure is insufficient to process a data topology (e.g., an unresolvable conflict that paralyzes the validation agenda), the system must stop synthesis and escalate. Escalation implies a direct transfer to human review. Any human intervention will operate as a "traced override," which documents the operator's axiomatic decision, but does not erase or overwrite the original records of conflict or uncertainty that triggered the escalation.

## 16. Minimum illustrative examples

**1. High benchmark**
- *Incorrect:* "The site has a severe thermal leak, as its HVAC consumption exceeds the regional benchmark by 18%." (Benchmark used as diagnosis).
- *Correct:* "The 18% deviation from the regional benchmark opens a tension regarding HVAC performance that requires validating the integrity of the thermal envelope." (Benchmark used as validation trigger).

**2. Operational proxy**
- *Incorrect:* "The facility uses subcritical ammonia systems with high maintenance costs." (Plausible practice written as fact).
- *Correct:* "The facility's archetype is compatible with subcritical ammonia systems; this operational hypothesis requires physical validation of the compressors." (Plausible practice written as conditioned hypothesis).

**3. Strong composition**
- *Incorrect:* "The concurrence of building age, high OPEX, and deviated benchmark confirms the obsolescence of the cooling plant." (Multiple composed cases written as confirmation).
- *Correct:* "The convergence of age, OPEX, and benchmark concentrates evidence gaps and prioritizes the inspection of the cooling plant in the validation agenda." (Composed convergence written as tension or validation priority).

**4. Conflict**
- *Incorrect:* "Although sustainability reports indicate recent modernization, the high consumption suggests equipment operates inefficiently." (Conflict narratively smoothed over by assuming the cause).
- *Correct:* "There is a material conflict between the corporate claim of modernization (Case A) and the energy consumption signature (Case B). Both cases are degraded to hypotheses in tension and escalated as maximum priority for empirical validation." (Conflict preserved and escalated).

**5. Sovereign LLM**
- *Incorrect:* "Considering the context, it is most likely that the problem lies in a logic controller failure, so I suggest reviewing the PLC." (LLM invents activation and resolves conflict without basis).
- *Correct:* "`inference_case_register` #42 conditions performance to PLC configuration; this dependency is transferred to the `validation_queue`." (LLM reformulates pre-existing structure).

## 17. Typical errors that 2D must block
Subphase 2D is operationally designed to intercept and abort the following design flaws:
- Benchmark used as local proof or physical diagnosis.
- Structural proxy used as operational confirmation.
- Analytical hypothesis written syntactically as a fact.
- Composition of multiple weak signals used as a mathematical multiplier of certainty.
- Narrative producing structure or inference non-existent in upstream objects.
- LLM closing conflicts through probabilistic intuition averages.
- Uncertainty erased from final outputs out of a desire for apparent clarity.
- Logical conflict smoothed over by writing style.
- Use of strong, causal, or prescriptive language before site truth collection.
- Human override used to "clean" or remove uncomfortable or contradictory outputs from the history.

## 18. Exact decisions that 2D closes
- The absolute prohibition of asymmetrical inferential leaps is regulated.
- The rules against aggregation of certainty are institutionalized.
- The restricted grammar of Phase 2 is definitive and not subject to stylistic preferences.
- The subordination of the LLM to structured objects is unalterable.
- The preservation of uncertainty and conflict is assumed as a survival mandate of the pipeline.

## 19. Completion criteria for 2D
Subphase 2D is considered complete when all restrictive policies (anti-leaps, conflict/uncertainty preservation, prohibited vocabulary, and LLM limits) are formally integrated into the Phase 2 pipeline, guaranteeing that it is logistically impossible for an inferential object to generate a downstream output that exceeds the evidentiary strength dictated by its structured inputs.

# 2E — Closing Gate, Minimum Tests, and Phase 2 Handoff

## 1. Exact purpose of the subphase
The purpose of subphase 2E is to establish the objective, auditable, and binary conditions that determine whether the conceptual and normative architecture of Phase 2 is formally closed, epistemologically secure, and ready for its technical materialization. Its sole objective is to audit and seal the design to prevent a false closure.

## 2. Why this subphase matters
A false closure occurs when a phase seems complete at a narrative level, but lacks strict boundaries, logical contracts, and verifiable definitions. If Phase 2 is closed with logical ambiguities, downstream software engineers (Implementation Phase) will be forced to invent heuristics, make epistemological decisions, and patch logical holes through code, forcing engineering to make epistemological decisions that do not belong to it. 2E matters because it ensures that the design is computable before computation exists.

## 3. Exact function of 2E
To act as the final layer of conceptual quality assurance. Phase 2E does not add new inferential capacity; it subjects everything defined in 2A, 2B, 2C, and 2D to a battery of structural validation. It sets the design acceptance criteria, defines which parts of the model remain immutable, and authorizes the handoff to the data and backend engineering teams.

## 4. What 2E does NOT do
- It does NOT redesign subphases 2A, 2B, 2C, or 2D.
- It does NOT introduce new analytical entities, scores, or inferential admissibility gates.
- It does NOT start software implementation or code writing.
- It does NOT define technical schemas (JSON, SQL, databases).
- It does NOT invade the operational functions of empirical validation (Phase 3) or final reporting (Phase 4).

## 5. What exactly this subphase must close
1. The undeniable list of conceptual artifacts and structured objects that must logically exist at the end of Phase 2.
2. The minimum (conceptual) test cases that the design must be able to resolve without violating its own rules.
3. The minimum set of binary gates (closing gates) that evaluate the completeness of the Master Document.
4. The hard ruling rules: pass, rebuild required, and catastrophic failure (no pass).
5. The exact perimeter of what technical "handoff" means.
6. The catalog of definitions that are frozen and the catalog of what remains flexible for engineering.

## 6. Mandatory artifacts at the close of Phase 2
For Phase 2 to be considered closed, naming the following objects is not enough; their logic of population, inheritance, and degradation must be univocally defined in the Master Document:
- `inference_case_register` (The immutable root repository of every activated case).
- `hypothesis_register` (The subset of claims about plausible states).
- `tension_map` (The map of operational or business frictions).
- `conflict_register` (The register of hard incompatibilities unresolvable by inference).
- `opportunity_candidate_matrix` (The strictly conditioned improvement pathways).
- `uncertainty_register` (The map of plural open states).
- `evidence_gap_register` (The material blockages due to lack of data).
- `validation_queue` (The top-down prioritization of empirical requirements).
- `next_best_questions` (The translation to concrete audit actions).

## 7. Minimum test cases that Phase 2 must pass
The analytical design must demonstrate, on paper or diagram, that its logic correctly processes the following extreme scenarios without violating Phase 0. These test cases are tests of conceptual robustness of the design, not empirical site validations:

- **Building / compliance-driven:** Demonstrate how an external regulatory pressure interacts with an empty data prior, forcing the activation of a risk case that translates exclusively into urgency in the `validation_queue`, without diagnosing non-compliance.
- **Industrial / reliability-driven:** Demonstrate how the pipeline intercepts a generic `opportunity_candidate` (e.g., shutting down equipment) and collides it against an `improvement_constraint_profile` (e.g., 24/7 uptime), automatically degrading or blocking it before it reaches an output register.
- **Steam / process heat (Depth):** Demonstrate the activation of a deep technical inference chain based on vintage and thermal archetype, ensuring that the final claim demands a physical `validation_requirement` (e.g., ultrasound on traps) and does not collapse into a theoretical savings calculation.
- **Sparse data case (Preserved uncertainty):** Demonstrate the system's behavior when the `facility_prior` lacks 80% of the data. The system must massively populate the `evidence_gap_register` and the `uncertainty_register` instead of forcing latent activations without support (zero compensatory hallucination).
- **High-conflict case (Escalation):** Demonstrate the logical resolution when two input bundles assert mutually exclusive truths (e.g., "100% electric heating" vs. "massive gas consumption in summer"). The design must prove that the case enters the `conflict_register` and aborts the generation of opportunities until Phase 3 resolves the collision.

## 8. Binary acceptance gates of Phase 2
The Master Document is only approved if all of the following boolean variables return `TRUE`:
- `phase2_scope_closed`: Is final diagnosis, ROI calculation, and holistic reporting explicitly prohibited?
- `inference_unit_closed`: Does the `Inference Case` have a minimum non-negotiable contract that includes `base_support` and `validation_requirement`?
- `library_logic_closed`: Is the strict mechanics that separates an inert latent case from an activated case defined?
- `structured_outputs_closed`: Is the semantics and inheritance relationship of the 9 mandatory artifacts unequivocal?
- `survival_logic_closed`: Are there explicit rules for degradation, blockage, and quick death for redundant or invalid cases?
- `epistemic_control_closed`: Are the prohibitions against epistemic leaps (proxy → truth, hypothesis → conclusion, etc.) and LLM sovereignty documented?
- `tests_defined`: Can the design trace the flow of the 5 minimum test cases without contradictions?
- `no_phase3_or_4_leakage`: Does the pipeline stop cleanly at the formulation of the validation agenda and preparatory structuring?
- `handoff_boundary_closed`: Is it clear what software engineers can and cannot alter?
- `document_master_is_actionable`: Does the document lack abstract narrative and provide deterministic rules that a developer can translate to code?

## 9. Pass / rebuild / no pass rule
- **PASS:** The 10 binary gates are `TRUE`. The design is coherent, restrictive, and actionable. Formal handoff to engineering is authorized.
- **REBUILD REQUIRED (Partial reconstruction):** One or more gates fail due to procedural ambiguity or omission (e.g., scores were defined but not their non-compensation, or the `Inference Case` lacks a clear degradation state). Requires returning to the specific subphase, patching the logical hole, and submitting for review again.
- **NO PASS (Catastrophic failure):** The design contains systemic violations to Phase 0 (e.g., uses the LLM as final judge, produces confirmed diagnoses, uses benchmarks as local evidence). Requires discarding the Phase 2 architecture and restarting from 2A.

## 10. What formal Phase 2 handoff means
Formal handoff does NOT mean that software implementation is finished, or even started. It means that the conceptual, semantic, and normative stratum has reached a solidity that allows engineering to build the infrastructure (schemas, tables, validators, pipelines, microservices, state machines, unit tests) without having to make decisions about how the knowledge domain works. Engineering receives a binding contract, not a draft of ideas.

## 11. What should be frozen upon closing Phase 2
Upon issuing the 2E pass, the following elements are frozen and cannot be altered without formally reopening the phase (Version Bump):
- The restrictive purpose (preparatory, not terminal).
- The atomic analytical unit (`Inference Case`).
- The operation paradigm (Governed inferential library vs. free reasoning).
- The minimum conceptual typology of claims.
- The semantics of structured output objects.
- The activation mechanics by triggers.
- The rules of composition and prohibition of epistemic inflation.
- The minimum scoring model (only 3 allowed) and the gate non-compensation rule.
- The differential survival taxonomy.
- Epistemological control policies (language, preservation of uncertainty/conflict).
- The minimum robustness test cases.

## 12. What can still change later without reopening Phase 2
The engineering team retains operational flexibility within the frozen semantic limits, to modify:
- Technical names of fields or variables (e.g., from `validation_queue` to `validation_queue_v2` or `val_q`).
- Table structure in relational or graph databases.
- Architecture of services and microservices.
- Technical orchestration, DAGs, and computational tooling.
- Physical partitioning strategies, message queues, and caching.
- Optimization of performance, latency, and computational costs (LLM routing).

## 13. Minimum illustrative examples
