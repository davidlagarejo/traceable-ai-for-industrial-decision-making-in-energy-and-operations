# 03_Claim_Status_Bridge

This vault uses the following compact bridge between constitutional status and front-of-product use:

| Constitutional support state | Front meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `unsupported` | do not surface as a finding |
| `hypothesis` | may surface only in technical or internal contexts |
| `indication` | may surface as a tension or signal |
| `decision_grade` | may shape prioritization, diligence, and no-go logic |
| `verification_grade` | may support the strongest bounded client-facing pages |
