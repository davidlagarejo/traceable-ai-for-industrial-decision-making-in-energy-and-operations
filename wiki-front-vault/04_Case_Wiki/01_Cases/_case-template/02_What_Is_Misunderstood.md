# What Is Misunderstood

Use this page to capture:

- what part of the asset appears poorly understood
- which system boundaries remain weak
- what the current reading cannot yet support

## Minimum content

- misunderstood subsystem or boundary
- why the misunderstanding matters
- what decision it contaminates
- strongest current support
- what evidence would materially sharpen the picture

## What this page does not close

- action priority by itself
- verified diagnosis
