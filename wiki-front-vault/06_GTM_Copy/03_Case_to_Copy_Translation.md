# Case to Copy Translation

Use this layer to translate from:

- evidence and case truth

into:

- website case pages
- LinkedIn reveals
- category explainers
- buyer-facing narratives

## Translation rule

No GTM translation may:

- erase uncertainty that still matters
- turn a screening claim into closure
- present probabilistic finance as verified economics
- or imply compliance closure without support

Good translation should prefer:

- tensions
- examples
- comparisons
- simple but deep language

It should avoid:

- academic wording
- hype wording
- empty AI language
- overexplaining the phase architecture

## Translation sequence

Start from:

1. what is misunderstood
2. what decision is at risk
3. what fragile assumption matters
4. what should not be done yet
5. what should be verified first
6. what preliminary exposure exists

Then translate into:

- a category statement
- a case-page thesis
- a website explanation
- a LinkedIn reveal

without changing support state.

## Hard prohibition

Do not write copy that makes the product sound like:

- a generic AI assistant
- an industrial chatbot
- a universal optimization engine
- a compliance certifier
- a verified financial underwriting engine
