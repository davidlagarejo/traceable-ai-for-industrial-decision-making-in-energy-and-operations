# GTM Index

This layer is a translation layer.

It exists to convert case truth into:

- category language
- buyer language
- positioning
- case-page language
- and reveal/copy surfaces

It is downstream of case and evidence pages.

It should not overexplain the internal phase architecture.

Its job is not to simplify by deleting uncertainty.

Its job is to translate:

- why this category matters
- why current diligence flows fail
- why the initial product exists before heavier work
- why the system is rigorous without pretending to be final

Do not use this layer as a substitute for case truth.
