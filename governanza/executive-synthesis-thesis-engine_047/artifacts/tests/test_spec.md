# Test Spec — Executive Synthesis / Thesis Engine

Motor ID: motor_047

## happy_path

- One Vanderbilt yields an admissible structural thesis with a dominant contradiction, bounded actions and bounded interpretive signals.
- contradiction ranking and rejected candidates remain visible after selection.
- congruence-bridge cases enrich the thesis with misunderstanding, boundary, comparison, loss, measurement, regulatory and finance takes.

## sparse_case

- target-classification or otherwise structurally inadmissible cases emit `inadmissible_thesis` with empty structural payload.
- empty structural TAD can be replaced by bounded congruence action priority when the thesis itself remains admissible.

## malformed_input

- missing or weak structural inputs must not create false contradiction certainty;
- the thesis may not pick a stronger report mode than the selected visible mode;
- congruence fields may not stay populated when the thesis is inadmissible.

## edge_cases

- logistics-style invalid comparison still produces hidden system-boundary error;
- infrastructure-style invalid comparison still produces the same bounded executive logic;
- false-CAPEX logic and boundary-misalignment signals remain explicit when supported.

## pass_criteria

- `executive_thesis` stays singular, bounded and reconstructable;
- counters match the thesis payload;
- direct hierarchy and congruence-bridge tests pass.

## fail_criteria

- inadmissible cases receive a polished structural thesis;
- contradiction ranking disappears;
- top actions or congruence takes outrun the evidence posture.
