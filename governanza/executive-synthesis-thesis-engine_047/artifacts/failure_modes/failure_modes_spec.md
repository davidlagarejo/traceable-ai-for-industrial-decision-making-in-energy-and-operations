## anti_patterns

- turning the thesis into a summary of every upstream register;
- forcing structural language onto target-classification-only cases;
- hiding rejected contradictions so the chosen contradiction looks unchallenged;
- translating bounded structural finance into investable certainty.

## degradation_signals

- more than a few top actions appear regularly;
- `surprising_but_evidenced_takeaway` becomes generic and detached from the chosen contradiction;
- congruence fields remain populated in inadmissible cases;
- rejected contradiction candidates disappear while conflict inputs remain plural.

## expensive_errors

- a persuasive executive thesis that points the client at the wrong problem;
- a compressed report hierarchy built on a false contradiction;
- a downstream outline that sounds certain while the value boundary is still unproven.
