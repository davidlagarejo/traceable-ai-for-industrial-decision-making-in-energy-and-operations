# Conceptual Schema — Executive Synthesis / Thesis Engine

Motor ID: motor_047

## thesis_model

The engine emits one `executive_thesis` object with two possible states:

- `admissible_structural_thesis`
- `inadmissible_thesis`

The second state is not an error. It is the correct bounded output when the case has not crossed the structural threshold.

## core_dimensions

An admissible thesis combines four dimensions:

1. problem reframe;
2. dominant contradiction and dominant lens;
3. bounded actionability;
4. interpretive or congruence-specific takes.

The thesis is therefore both a summary and a governor. It decides what the report should sound like without exceeding the evidence.

## selection_logic

The engine must preserve its own selection basis:

- ranked conflict candidates;
- rejected contradiction candidates;
- dominant contradiction selection basis;
- top variables, top scenarios and top actions.

This matters because the thesis is not supposed to look magically obvious. It must remain reconstructable from upstream structural evidence.

## congruence_bridge

When congruence intelligence is present and the thesis is structurally admissible, the engine may bridge:

- dominant operational misunderstanding;
- hidden system boundary error;
- invalid comparison risk;
- loss logic;
- measurement minimality take;
- regulatory physics take;
- finance-to-physics take;
- maintenance reality take.

These fields should enrich the thesis, not replace the structural center of gravity.
