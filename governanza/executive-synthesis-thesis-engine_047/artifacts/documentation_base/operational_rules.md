# Operational Rules — Executive Synthesis / Thesis Engine

Motor ID: motor_047

## rule_1_no_structural_threshold_no_structural_thesis

If the canonical problem frame is inactive or the case is still effectively target-classification-only, the engine must emit `inadmissible_thesis` with empty structural contradiction and empty action payloads.

## rule_2_pick_one_dominant_contradiction

The engine must select one dominant contradiction and preserve the ranking basis behind that choice. It may not flatten multiple contradictory theses into one vague executive paragraph.

## rule_3_client_facing_action_budget_is_small

The thesis may surface only a few top actions. These actions must remain bounded, evidence-led and compatible with the structural TAD and the prohibited-action surface.

## rule_4_financial_logic_stays_bounded

Capital logic may be explained in bounded terms, but the thesis may not jump from structural exposure to ROI closure, savings certainty or underwriting-grade recommendation.

## rule_5_congruence_bridge_is_optional_and_conditional

Congruence-specific fields should appear only when congruence intelligence is present and the structural thesis is admissible. In inadmissible cases, these fields must collapse back to empty strings or empty registers.

## rule_6_interpretive_signals_must_be-evidenced

Interpretive signals such as boundary misalignment or false CAPEX logic must remain tied to the chosen contradiction and evidence state. They are not free-form rhetoric.

## rule_7_report_mode_must_stay-aligned

The thesis `report_mode` must stay aligned with the selected visible output mode from `motor_034`. The thesis cannot secretly promote itself to a stronger client-facing mode.
