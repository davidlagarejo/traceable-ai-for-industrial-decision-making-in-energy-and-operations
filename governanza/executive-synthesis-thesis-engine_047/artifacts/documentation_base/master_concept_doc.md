# Master Concept Doc — Executive Synthesis / Thesis Engine

Motor ID: motor_047

## core_job

`motor_047` condenses the structural lane into one bounded executive thesis. It is the place where contradictions, dominant variables, minimum evidence, financial exposure, peer framing, redesign logic and congruence-specific takes are compressed into a single client-facing interpretive center of gravity.

Its output is not the whole report. It is the disciplined thesis that the rest of the report must follow.

## why_it_exists

Before this motor, the framework has many valid but separate surfaces:

- canonical problem frame;
- dominant variables;
- cross-layer contradictions;
- scenario space;
- structural financial exposure;
- minimum evidence for discrimination;
- congruence invalid-comparison logic;
- measurement minimality, regulatory physics and finance/physics dependency.

Without a thesis engine, the report risks becoming a list of motors. `motor_047` prevents that by forcing the system to choose a single bounded contradiction, a single reframed problem, a small action set and a small set of interpretive signals.

## behavioral_contract

- produce one `executive_thesis` object;
- keep it bounded and admissible under the current evidence state;
- emit an inadmissible thesis when the case has not crossed the structural-problem threshold;
- preserve ranked conflict candidates and rejected contradictions instead of hiding the selection logic;
- bridge congruence-specific insights into the thesis only when the case is structurally admissible.

## non_goals

- it does not write the full report body;
- it does not expand to unlimited recommendations;
- it does not override blocked claims or prohibited financial outputs;
- it does not force structural interpretation onto target-classification-only cases.

## downstream_role

`motor_047` is the executive semantic anchor for `motor_048`, the structural executive summary and the client-facing hierarchy checks enforced later by `motor_036`. If this motor drifts, the whole report hierarchy drifts.
