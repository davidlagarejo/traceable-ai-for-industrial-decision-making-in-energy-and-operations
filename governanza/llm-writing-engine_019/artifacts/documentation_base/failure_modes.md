# Failure Modes — LLM Writing Engine

Motor ID: motor_019

## primary_failures

- `PACKET_ESCAPE`: the prose mentions facts or numbers not present in `source_facts`.
- `FRAME_DRIFT`: the writer slips into underwriting, due diligence or transaction framing.
- `LINT_BYPASS`: hard-closure language, forbidden phrases or unsupported numbers survive into final output.
- `HIDDEN_FALLBACK`: fallback or structured-summary rendering happens without being surfaced in governance metadata.
- `MATURITY_ERASURE`: blocked claims and readiness bottlenecks disappear from the narrative contract.

## why_these_failures_matter

This motor is the most temptation-prone LLM surface in the framework. If it drifts, the report can sound more certain than the runtime actually is. That would turn a writing layer into an unauthorized analysis layer.

## required_response

The correct response to writing drift is not to trust better prompting. It is to restore packet discipline, lint discipline and fallback transparency.
