# Runtime Executive Thesis V2 Execution Plan

Generated on: `2026-04-30`

## Objective

Execute the next hardening wave so ZLab stops merely being:

`correct + structured + bounded`

and becomes:

`sharp + hierarchical + interpretively uncomfortable + reality-revealing`

This execution plan is not about adding more analysis blocks.
It is about making the system surface what is most surprising, most economically dangerous, and most decision-relevant in the subject reality.

The core standard is:

- the report must not merely summarize evidence
- it must identify the hidden structural mistake the client is at risk of making
- it must explain why that mistake is economically dangerous
- it must define the minimum evidence that would prove or kill that interpretation

---

## Strategic Upgrade

### What the next version must do better

The framework is already good at:

- bounding claims
- refusing fake ROI
- separating observed vs conditional vs archetypal
- forcing structural-first body hierarchy
- demoting full registers to appendices

The next upgrade must make it better at:

- selecting the one contradiction that matters most
- exposing the non-obvious economic boundary error
- surfacing when the client is solving the wrong problem
- distinguishing “interesting” from “decision-dominant”
- making peer comparisons sharper and less generic
- making redesign logic economically legible, not just technically plausible
- making the system visibly interpret reality, not just serialize motor outputs

---

## The Standard Of “What Must Stand Out”

Every strong case must make at least one of these realities explicit in the body:

1. The client is optimizing the wrong variable.
2. The visible benchmark is not the real bottleneck.
3. The cost bearer and the control holder are misaligned.
4. The proposed CAPEX logic fails if the economic boundary is different from the physical boundary.
5. The process may be structurally intensive rather than operationally wasteful.
6. A peer looks better for a reason that may not transfer.
7. The real value-of-information is not “more data”; it is one discriminating evidence pack.

This is the layer that must “surprise” the user:

- not theatrically
- not speculatively
- but by revealing the hidden structural mistake in the current framing

---

## Precision Criteria

### A. Reality Interpretation Precision

The main thesis must answer, explicitly:

- what is the client assuming?
- what hidden structural assumption may be false?
- what contradiction makes that assumption dangerous?
- what economic logic changes if the contradiction resolves the other way?

### B. Executive Force Precision

The first page must forcefully state:

- the reframed problem
- the dominant contradiction
- the immediate admissible action
- the prohibited action
- the financial exposure if the framing is wrong

### C. Non-Obviousness Precision

The thesis fails if it is still readable as:

- “more data is needed”
- “there are multiple uncertainties”
- “benchmarking suggests opportunity”
- “peer comparison suggests best practices”

Instead it must sound like:

- “this is not an energy problem yet”
- “this is a control-boundary problem”
- “this is not an efficiency question until the load-capture boundary is resolved”
- “this CAPEX logic may fail even if the building is real, large, and regulated”

---

## P0 Outcomes

This execution wave is complete only if all five outcomes are true:

2. The body contains only thesis-relevant material.

## ETV2-05 — Appendix Non-Competition Contract

### Problem

We already found this in production: appendix sections can still compete with body authority by repeating the same topic.

### Goal

Appendices must support the thesis, never visually or semantically rival it.

### Owner

- [motor_016.py](/Volumes/ZLab_Run/Zlab_Run/Repos/zlab-operational-truth-framework/runtime-orchestrator/src/runtime_orchestrator/adapters/motor_016.py)
- [motor_036.py](/Volumes/ZLab_Run/Zlab_Run/Repos/zlab-operational-truth-framework/runtime-orchestrator/src/runtime_orchestrator/adapters/motor_036.py)

### Rules

- no appendix may share the exact visible title of a body section
- technical extension sections must be named `"... — Technical Register"` or `"... — Technical Appendix"`
- if appendix content is semantically equivalent to body content, it must be narrower, deeper, or evidentiary, not duplicative

### Done when

- appendix titles are non-competing
- appendix sections extend the body instead of restating it

### Tests

- `test_appendix_titles_do_not_compete_with_body_titles`
- `test_appendix_equivalent_sections_are_disambiguated`
