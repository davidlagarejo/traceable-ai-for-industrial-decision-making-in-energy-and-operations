## Anti-Hallucination Rules

These rules are now implemented or enforced:

1. No `ARCHETYPAL_PRIOR` may be presented as `OBSERVED_FACT`.
2. Every emitted claim must have a contract row in `claim_contract_register`.
3. Every scenario with financial meaning must have `evidence_needed` and `falsification_condition`.
4. Structural financial output may not emit `ROI`, `IRR`, `NPV`, `payback`, `bankability`, or fake savings without maturity.
5. Benchmarking cannot be used as local truth without control-boundary and evidence context.
6. TAD cannot promote actions above the claim-permission ceiling.
7. Structural sections cannot render if the validator finds missing required sections or unsupported claims.
