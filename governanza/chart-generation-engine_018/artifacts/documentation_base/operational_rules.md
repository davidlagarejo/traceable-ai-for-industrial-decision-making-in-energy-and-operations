# Operational Rules — Chart Generation Engine

Motor ID: motor_018

## rule_1_all_charts_are_taxonomy_governed

Every chart asset must carry category, lane, intent and catalog-version metadata.

## rule_2_copy_changes_with_mode

Blocked, exploratory and structural report modes must produce different curation copy when the chart semantics change with the report's epistemic posture.

## rule_3_case_context_is_mandatory

Charts must be stamped with current-case context and same-case match state before they are allowed downstream.

## rule_4_visuals_remain_claim_subordinate

Charts may clarify evidence, contradiction, gap structure or search logic, but they may not imply stronger conclusions than the upstream governance surfaces support.

## rule_5_supporting_charts_stay_in_their_lane

Gap taxonomy, next-best-search, peer readiness and measurement minimality charts should support validation or contradiction logic rather than posing as final decision charts.
