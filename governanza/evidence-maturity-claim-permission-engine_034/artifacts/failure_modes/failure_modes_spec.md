# Failure Modes Spec — Evidence Maturity & Claim Permission Engine

Motor ID: motor_034

## failure_modes_list

- `CRITICAL_FIELD_FALSE_ALLOW`: missing critical variables still unlock strong claims or decisions.
- `DECLARED_INPUT_ESCAPE`: declared-input rows exceed the intended maturity ceiling.
- `BENCHMARK_OVERTRUST`: benchmark-only signals unlock asset-specific strong conclusions.
- `DATASET_SHAPE_OVERREACH`: accepted dataset metadata upgrades maturity without an observed field value.
- `REGULATORY_SCOPE_LEAK`: LL97-specific claims appear outside NYC.
- `STRUCTURAL_PRIMARY_OVERPROMOTION`: active structural framing incorrectly upgrades the primary report.
- `CANONICAL_FRAME_MISS`: structural lane is bound but `canonical_problem_frame` remains inactive.
- `READINESS_SUMMARY_DRIFT`: summary counts or allowed/prohibited report types no longer match the registers.

## anti_patterns

- using requested report type as a direct authorization instead of a proposal;
- treating a plausible field value as mature evidence without source discipline;
- collapsing structural secondary outputs into primary report promotion without a separate gate;
- hiding blocked claims instead of exposing why they remain blocked.

## degradation_signals

- too many variables bunch at the highest maturity without strong sources;
- `report_type_allowed` and classifier tables disagree;
- structural output modes activate broadly while claim permissions remain thin or empty;
- non-NYC cases mention LL97 in claim names or evidence packs.

## expensive_errors

- overstated ROI, compliance or technical recommendations;
- suppressed structural framing that hides the real problem;
- output-mode selection that looks coherent but is no longer evidence-admissible;
- downstream synthesis speaking more strongly than the evidence base actually permits.
