# Operational Rules — System Consistency Validator

Motor ID: motor_036

## rule_1_visible_report_mode_must_match_authoritative_mode

The visible document type, the compressed outline mode and the executive thesis report mode must all describe the same admissible output. If those surfaces diverge, the report package is not render-safe.

## rule_2_claim_surfaces_must_reconcile

Claim summaries, claim matrices, claim contracts and visible claim traces must stay in sync. The system may not present a visible statement that lacks a supporting claim contract or a visible claim summary that contradicts the authoritative matrix.

## rule_3_client_facing_body_must_stay_compressed_and_clean

Client-facing body sections must respect the bounded render contract. Raw technical registers, duplicate sections or appendix-grade material may not leak back into the primary body. The body is thesis-driven, not dump-driven.

## rule_4_structural_and_congruence_outputs_must_remain_bounded

Invalid comparison risks, loss-pattern hypotheses, measurement strategy, hardware minimality, finance-physics dependencies and strategic action surfaces must stay within their bounded evidence posture. They may not masquerade as observed local fact or unrestricted operational proof.

## rule_5_declared_input_and_entity_conflicts_block_render

Declared-input rows may not be promoted as verified evidence. Unresolved entity-resolution conflicts, foreign entity labels, conflicted boundaries or foreign chart case fingerprints are render-blocking conditions.

## rule_6_chart_and_chapter_assets_must_match_the_current_case

Chart assets must carry current-case context, and planned chapter inventories must match the resolved render sections while excluding template scaffolding. A polished foreign or stale asset is still an integrity failure.

## rule_7_tad_and_scenario_surfaces_must_match_the_authoritative_registers

Scenario sections, TAD content and expanded structural actions must reflect the actual evidence and permission state. If the visible TAD implies a stronger action than the authoritative decision or claim surfaces allow, render must stop.

## rule_8_render_gate_is_binary

This motor does not score on a continuum. If one critical rule fails, `can_render_pdf` must remain false. The package is either coherent enough to render or it is not.
